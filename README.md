# Ke-Jie Kuo 郭科頡

M.S. student at the Graduate Institute of Advanced Semiconductor Technology, National Taiwan University of Science and Technology.

## Research focus

- Silicon photonics, semiconductor optoelectronics, and high-frequency measurement
- Reproducible research workflows that connect sample conditions, instrument settings, raw data, analysis versions, and conclusions
- Research software and cloud-based benchmark environments

## Selected work

- Led the architecture, deployment, and acceptance work for the [NTUST SiPh Lab website and CMS](https://ntust-siph-lab-105924420674.asia-east1.run.app/).
- Completed a 2.45 GHz transmission-line project with S11, S12, S21, and S22 analysis, plus microwave-antenna exposure, cleaning, milling, and soldering practice.
- Third author of a Mandarin-English mixed ASR manuscript in preparation for submission to IEEE TCDS. My scope covers containerized inference, the GKE Standard and V100 benchmark environment, data checks, staged benchmark execution, and resource shutdown.
- Developing Laboratory Innovation Brain as a traceable research-evidence system. The M0a evidence-identity foundation is complete, M0b is in progress, and Silicon Photonics is the first planned research domain.
